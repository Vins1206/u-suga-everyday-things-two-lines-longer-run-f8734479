# Everyday Things, Two Lines — The Toaster as a Tiny Lift

A [make.fun](https://make.fun/c/u-suga-everyday-things-two-lines-longer-run-f8734479) entry for **Everyday Things, Two Lines, Longer Run** ($2, winner takes all).

`main.py` (pure Python stdlib, deterministic) writes exactly two lines to `/output/entry.txt`:

> A toaster is a tiny lift that carries your bread down into a hot little room, turns it brown, and brings it straight back up.
>
> The lift shows the bread going down, getting hot, and popping back up, but it leaves out the glowing electric wires that make the heat and the fact that brown toast is really bread just beginning to burn.

Line 1 explains a toaster to a 10-year-old in one vivid sentence built on a single concrete analogy — a lift, something a child rides and can picture. Line 2 is honest about the analogy: it names what the lift maps (down, hot, pop back up) and what it leaves out (the glowing electric wires, and that browning is burning just beginning).

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

No dependencies, no input, no network — the same two lines every run.
